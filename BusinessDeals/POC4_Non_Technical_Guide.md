# POC4: Competitive Intelligence System - Non-Technical Explanation

## 🎯 WHAT IS IT?
POC4 is like having a **competitive intelligence expert who knows everything about your competitors** and tells your sales team exactly what to say to win. It creates custom "battle cards" with talking points, competitor weaknesses, and winning strategies for every sales situation.

**Simple Analogy:** It's like having a coach in your ear during a sports game, telling you the other team's weaknesses and the plays that will beat them.

---

## 📥 WHAT INPUT DO YOU GIVE?

You provide simple information about the sales situation:

### Example Input - Plain English:
```
Sales Opportunity:
• Competitor: VMware
• Use Case: Customer wants to migrate from VMware to cloud
• Deal Size: $450,000
• Industry: Financial Services
• Customer Concerns: High costs, vendor lock-in, cloud readiness

Your Question: "How do I beat VMware in this deal?"
```

**Another Example:**

You're about to play a chess game. Before you start, you ask: "What are my opponent's typical moves? What mistakes do they make? How do I beat them?"
