# POC2: Personalized Demo Creator - Non-Technical Explanation

## 🎯 WHAT IS IT?
POC2 is like having a **personal assistant who creates custom product demonstrations** tailored to each customer. Instead of showing everyone the same generic demo, this tool automatically creates a personalized experience based on who's watching and what they care about.

---

## 📥 WHAT INPUT DO YOU GIVE?

You provide simple information about the person watching the demo:

### Example Input - Plain English:
```
Name: Sarah Chen
Title: Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
Company: FinTech Startup

Concerns: "Our cloud bill grew 300% last year with no visibility into 
          where the money is going. We need better cost control."

Industry: Financial Services (ecommerce)
Technology Stack: Multi-cloud, Microservices, Databricks
```

**Another Example:**
```
Name: Mike Rodriguez
Title: Security Lead
Company: Healthcare Provider

Concerns: "We need to pass HIPAA compliance audits and protect patient 
          data from ransomware attacks."

Industry: Healthcare
```

**Think of it like:**
- Going to a car dealership and the salesperson asks: "What's important to you?"
- CFO says: "Fuel efficiency and total cost of ownership"
- Security person says: "Safety features and reliability"
- They show you different things based on your answer!
