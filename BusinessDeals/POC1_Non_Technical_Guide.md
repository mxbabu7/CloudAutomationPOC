# POC1: Smart Infrastructure Designer - Non-Technical Explanation

## 🎯 WHAT IS IT?
POC1 is like having an **expert architect who designs technology solutions instantly**. Just like an architect designs a building based on your needs, this tool designs the technology infrastructure your business needs - but does it in minutes instead of weeks.

---

## 📥 WHAT INPUT DO YOU GIVE?

You provide simple business information (no technical knowledge needed):

### Example Input - Plain English:
```
Company: FinTech Startup
Description: "We need a highly available payment processing system with 
             AI fraud detection. Must comply with PCI-DSS and GDPR. 
             Budget constrained but need 99.99% uptime."
             
Required Uptime: 99.99% (almost never down)
Monthly Budget: $10,000
Timeline: 6 weeks
Must Comply With: PCI-DSS (payment card security), GDPR (data privacy)
Sustainability Goal: Reduce carbon footprint by 30%
```

**Think of it like ordering a custom home:**
- "I need a 4-bedroom house" → "I need a payment processing system"
- "Near good schools" → "Must be 99.99% available"
- "Budget is $500K" → "Budget is $10,000/month"
- "Need it wheelchair accessible" → "Must comply with PCI-DSS and GDPR"
